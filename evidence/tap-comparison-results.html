What the flags mean

A settling flag means the average sensor reading in the final 20 ms differed from the starting baseline by more than 0.6 g. The hit was captured, but it is excluded from the clean comparison. This does not say the tap was too light or prove a lasting change of position.

The last front light window reached 1.85 g but never captured a pulse above the 2 g trigger. It is not a zero-g hit.
